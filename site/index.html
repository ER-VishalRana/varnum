How it works

Questions

Frequently asked

Is Varnum really free?

Yes. Writing, translating, templates, PDF and Word downloads and the file tools are all free.

Which languages does it support?

Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia, Urdu, Nepali, Sanskrit, English and more. You can also type Hindi and get English back.

Are my letters and files private?

Files you open (Excel, Word, PowerPoint, PDF) are processed on your device and are not uploaded. If you sign in, your saved letters and details are stored on the Varnum server so they sync between your devices.

Why is the app not on the Play Store?

It is coming. Until then you can install the APK from this page. Android will ask once to allow installing apps from your browser.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes, through the web app. Open varnum.alvvintechnology.com/app in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen.
